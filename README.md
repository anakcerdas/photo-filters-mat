# photo-filters-mat
Photo Filters Mat turns ordinary photos into prints, film frames and collages. Instead of one filter per photo, you stack as many effects as you like, reorder them, and adjust each one.

Print effects: risograph, halftone, paper cut-out collage with custom colours, tape and frame shapes, photocopy, grunge and dither.
Light effects: halation, bloom, motion glow, light streaks, star filters with 4, 6, 8 or 16 points, mist glow, light leaks, digicam flash and slow shutter.
Film looks: 36 presets styled after classic colour and black-and-white film, each with matching grain.
Colour and glitch: gradient maps, infrared pink, VHS tape, pixel sort and colour fringe.
Recipes: one-tap combinations such as Night drive, Zine print and Disposable.
Double exposure: blend up to three photos with 15 blend modes, and drag to position each layer.
Batch editing: open several photos, edit them one by one or all together, and copy and paste edits between them.
Flexible export: save at original size as JPG or PNG, export a whole batch as a ZIP, or save a layered PSD with one layer per effect.

Your photos stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and you keep all rights to what you make.
